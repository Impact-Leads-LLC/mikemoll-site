The Compression Method

Custom coaching that fixes your biggest problem first.

This is live coaching, built around you and your business. We find the one thing holding you back the most and fix it first. Once that heaviest problem moves, you get a win where it counts, and you finally get paid for the value you create.

Your biggest problem is always one of these three:

  • Marketing

    Authority that creates demand

    We sharpen who you're for and how you talk about it, and build the authority that brings the right buyers to you. Steady demand follows from that.

  • Sales

    More calls turn into clients

    We give you a sales process you run the same way every time: qualify the right people, run the same discovery, price it for you to win, and follow up so warm leads don't slip away.

  • The Offer

    A business that serves your life

    We shape your work into one clear outcome you can charge a premium for, then build the systems and support that take you off the critical path and give your time back.

We find the one thing causing the most friction and fix it, then move on to the next constraint.
