The Compression Method

A method built from years of fixing service businesses.

Every business has one constraint that slows everything else down. The Compression Method finds yours, fixes it first, and cuts the work that doesn't need to be there.

The constraint almost always sits in one of three places:

  • Marketing

    The right buyers, who already get it

    We sharpen who you're for and how you explain what you do, so the people who reach out already understand your offer before the first call.

  • Sales

    A sales process you can measure

    We turn your calls into one process you run the same way every time: qualify the right people, run the same discovery, price it for you to win, and follow up. It's simple enough to hand to your team.

  • The Offer

    An offer that's easy to buy and deliver

    We shape your work into one clear outcome you can charge a premium for, then cut the delivery steps that eat your time.

Once one constraint moves, we go after the next.
