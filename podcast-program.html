How the three months run

Most of the setup happens in weeks 1 and 2, so your first pitches are going out by week 3.

1Month 1

Build your kit and start pitching

  1. Week 1

    Your guest kit

    • A 2.5-hour brand interview.
    • From it, we write your speaker sheet and your pitch and follow-up emails.
    • A list of shows your buyers listen to. Before a show goes on it, we check that it still publishes and takes guests, and we note what the host looks for in a guest.
    • Verified contact details for each host, plus the host's and the show's social accounts.
    • Access to our outreach tool, with your campaigns set up.
  2. Week 2

    Your offer and lead magnet

    We review your offer and work out how to present it to podcast audiences. You also get a plan for your lead magnet and landing page, for your team to build.

  3. Week 3

    Media training

    You learn to tell your stories and come across well on a show.

  4. Week 4

    First bookings

    Your first bookings can come in this week. As part of your media training, we also go through any podcast or media interviews you've already done.

2Months 2 and 3

Book more shows

  • Pitches keep going out every week.
  • I review your recorded interviews and give you feedback.
  • Open office hours every Tuesday and Thursday, 1:00 to 2:30 pm Eastern. Bring strategy questions, changes to your offer and podcast materials, or your story and delivery to work on.
  • Before the three months end, we review your bookings and you decide whether to renew.

What I need from you

  • Your 2.5-hour brand interview in week 1, then time for media training and the office hours you want to join.
  • Links to any podcast or media interviews you've done.
  • Your team to build the lead magnet and landing page from our plan.
  • An hour on your calendar for each recording.
  • On Done-With-You, a VA or team member who can run your outreach each week.

How booking works

  • What counts as a booking

    A host has given you a recording date. On Done-For-You, bookings that come after day 90 from pitches sent during the program still count toward the average.

  • How long things take

    Month 1 has fewer bookings, because pitching starts partway through it. Hosts often take weeks to reply, record a few weeks after they book you, and publish weeks after that.

  • Show approval

    You see each show first and pick yes or skip before anyone pitches it.

  • A weekly update

    When your outreach runs in our tool, you get a weekly email with where each show stands and your next recording.
