TLDR

  • Online does not mean complicated. Clarify one offer and one place to reach buyers.
  • Show up where your buyers already spend time instead of trying to be everywhere.

I joined Zohaib and Matthew on the It's Simple podcast to talk about taking a business online. A lot of owners think going online means a bigger website, more channels, more tools. It usually means the opposite.

Get found where your buyers already are

You do not need to be on every platform. You need to be in the one or two places your buyers already spend time, showing up often enough that they remember you. Pick the room, learn what people there care about, and be useful in it. That beats a scattered presence across five channels you cannot keep up with.

Make the offer easy to say yes to

Most of what looks like a marketing problem online is really an offer problem. If people cannot tell in a sentence what you do and who it is for, no amount of content fixes that. Tighten the offer first. One clear outcome, one clear buyer. Then the content has something simple to point at, and the whole thing gets easier to sell.
